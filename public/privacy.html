Privacy policy

Outbox and Outbox Autofill · Last updated October 20, 2026

Outbox (tracker.haoyang.us) is a job-application tracker. Outbox Autofill is its Chrome extension: it fills job application forms from the profile you keep in Outbox and records each application you send. Both are an open-source project run by its developer. This page says what data they handle, where it goes, and how to remove it.

What Outbox keeps

You sign in to Outbox with your Google account, and Google gives Outbox your name, email address and profile picture. Only approved email addresses can sign in.

What you put in Outbox is stored under your account in its database on Cloudflare (Cloudflare D1):

  • your applications, the jobs and companies they're for, and your notes on them;
  • your resume versions and their PDF files;
  • your autofill profile: contact details, address, links, work authorization, education, work history, preferences, and the answers you choose for voluntary self-identification questions (such as gender, race, veteran and disability status).

What the extension does with your data

  • On a site it doesn't know, it only looks for an application form (a resume upload beside name or email fields). It does nothing else there unless it finds one or you click its icon.
  • To fill a form, it downloads your profile and the resume you pick from Outbox, using your Outbox sign-in, and keeps a copy in Chrome on your computer.
  • On an application page it reads the form's questions and the job posting, and fills in answers. It never submits a form: you do.
  • When you send an application, it sends Outbox the company, the job title, the posting's link, and the email address and resume version you used, so the application shows in your tracker. You can undo it.
  • It remembers answers you type into questions it couldn't fill, to offer them next time. They stay in Chrome on your computer.
  • "This page didn't fill right" saves the page's structure, with your answers and details taken out, in Chrome on your computer. It isn't sent anywhere.
  • Its settings, including any AI key you enter, stay in Chrome on your computer and are never sent to Outbox.

AI providers

AI is optional. If you add an API key in the extension's settings, the extension sends requests from your browser straight to the provider you chose (OpenAI or another OpenAI-compatible service, or Anthropic), with your key. A request holds what its answer needs: the question, the job posting, and the parts of your profile or resume that bear on it, or a resume's text when you ask to read it into your profile. That provider handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy. Without a key, nothing goes to an AI provider.

On the website, Resume tools can read a resume you choose into your profile. That runs on a service operated with Outbox, which sends the resume's text to OpenAI or Anthropic for that one request; the text is held in memory only and isn't saved. Discover reads public job boards and needs nothing of yours.

Who else receives data

  • Cloudflare hosts Outbox and its database.
  • Google provides sign-in.
  • The AI provider you choose, as described above.

Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used for anything but the features above. Outbox and the extension use no analytics, ads or tracking. Outbox Autofill's use of data follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete your applications, resumes and profile in Outbox at any time.
  • Delete remembered answers and saved reports in the extension's settings. Removing the extension deletes everything it kept in Chrome.
  • To have your Outbox account and everything in it deleted, ask through the contact below.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will say so, with a new date. For questions or requests, open an issue at github.com/heyemlee/work-tracker, or write to the email address on Outbox Autofill's Chrome Web Store page.

隐私政策

Outbox 与 Outbox Autofill · 最后更新于 2026 年 10 月 20 日

Outbox（tracker.haoyang.us）是一个求职申请追踪工具。Outbox Autofill 是它的 Chrome 扩展：用你在 Outbox 里保存的资料填写求职申请表，并记录你投出的每一份申请。两者都是由其开发者维护的开源项目。本页说明它们处理哪些数据、数据去向，以及如何删除。

Outbox 保存什么

你用 Google 账号登录 Outbox，Google 会提供你的姓名、邮箱和头像。只有获准的邮箱才能登录。

你存进 Outbox 的内容，保存在它位于 Cloudflare 的数据库（Cloudflare D1）中，归在你的账号下：

  • 你的申请记录、对应的职位和公司，以及你的备注；
  • 你的各个简历版本及其 PDF 文件；
  • 你的自动填写资料：联系方式、地址、链接、工作许可、教育经历、工作经历、偏好，以及你为自愿身份调查题（如性别、种族、退伍军人身份、残疾状况）选定的答案。

扩展如何使用你的数据

  • 在它不认识的网站上，它只查看页面上有没有申请表（简历上传框加上姓名或邮箱栏）。除非找到申请表或你点了它的图标，它什么都不做。
  • 填表时，它用你的 Outbox 登录状态，从 Outbox 下载你的资料和你选的简历，并在你电脑上的 Chrome 里留一份副本。
  • 在申请页面上，它读取表单的题目和职位描述，并填入答案。它从不替你提交，提交由你来点。
  • 你提交申请后，它把公司、职位名称、职位链接、你用的邮箱和简历版本发给 Outbox，让这份申请出现在你的追踪列表里。你可以撤销。
  • 它会记住你在它没填上的题目里亲手填的答案，下次再遇到时提供。这些答案只保存在你电脑上的 Chrome 里。
  • “这页没填好”会把页面的结构（已去掉你的答案和个人信息）保存在你电脑上的 Chrome 里，不会发送到任何地方。
  • 它的设置，包括你填入的 AI key，只保存在你电脑上的 Chrome 里，从不发送给 Outbox。

AI 服务商

AI 是可选的。如果你在扩展设置里填了 API key，扩展会用你的 key，从你的浏览器直接向你选的服务商（OpenAI 或其他兼容 OpenAI 的服务，或 Anthropic）发送请求。请求里只有回答所需的内容：题目、职位描述、你资料或简历中相关的部分；或者在你要求把简历读进资料时，发送简历的文字。服务商按它自己的条款和隐私政策处理这些数据。不填 key，就不会向任何 AI 服务商发送数据。

网站上的 Resume tools 可以把你选的简历读进资料。它运行在与 Outbox 配套的服务上，该服务只为这一次请求把简历文字发给 OpenAI 或 Anthropic；文字只留在内存里，不会保存。Discover 读取公开的招聘网站，不需要你的任何数据。

还有谁会收到数据

  • Cloudflare：托管 Outbox 及其数据库。
  • Google：提供登录。
  • 你选择的 AI 服务商，如上所述。

数据不会被出售，不会用于广告，也不会用于上述功能以外的任何用途。Outbox 和扩展都不使用任何统计、广告或追踪。Outbox Autofill 对数据的使用遵守Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策，包括其中的有限使用（Limited Use）要求。

你的选择

  • 你随时可以在 Outbox 里修改或删除你的申请、简历和资料。
  • 在扩展设置里可以删除记住的答案和保存的反馈记录。卸载扩展会删除它在 Chrome 里保存的所有内容。
  • 如需删除你的 Outbox 账号及其中的全部数据，请通过下方联系方式提出。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会在本页注明并更新日期。如有问题或请求，请在 github.com/heyemlee/work-tracker 提交 issue，或写信到 Outbox Autofill 在 Chrome 应用商店页面上列出的邮箱。
